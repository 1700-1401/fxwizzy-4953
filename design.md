## Fxwizzy — chart intelligence workspace
Responsive web application using the supplied logo as the visual reference. Dense but calm professional trading desk, not a marketing landing page.

### Tokens
- Background #0c0e12; sidebar #101216; panel #14171d; raised #1b1e25; border #292c34.
- Text #eceef2; secondary #9296a3; gold #e4c078; magenta #bc2868; emerald #48c59d; red #e07883.
- Gold is reserved for primary actions and selection. Magenta provides subtle brand atmosphere in the sidebar brand card. Green/red mean bullish/bearish, not successful/guaranteed.

### Typography and layout
- Poppins for body, Manrope for display, monospace for price numerals. Fonts self-hosted in public/fonts.
- 220px desktop sidebar, 72px app bar, 28px main padding. Main chart occupies two thirds, setup one third. Fine hairline borders, 10px radii, clear uppercase micro labels and spacious section titles.
- Gold feature mark, navigation icons, restrained transitions, faint dot pattern in upload surface. No decorative photos.
- Small screens collapse sidebar to a top brand and horizontal scrollable navigation; panels become one column.

### Screens and interaction
- Analyzer: symbol selectors, upload/capture modal, timeframe tabs, screenshot/sample canvas, contextual insight tabs, conditional setup, risk quick calculator.
- Journal: browser-local saved analyses and reopening results.
- Risk calculator: USD balance, risk %, entry, stop and contract units with explicit USD quote assumptions.
- Trading guide: grounded checklist; not fake live market news.
- Settings: local risk preferences and privacy information.

### Honesty rules
Initial illustrated chart and setup are explicitly SAMPLE / NOT LIVE. Real results replace sample after upload. No synthetic confidence probability. Only supplied chart timeframes are analyzed. Missing readable scales produces clarification instead of invented prices. No execution integration. No guaranteed-return claims.
